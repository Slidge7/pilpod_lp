02 — The S7 Standard

Seven commitments behind every release.

Not a manifesto. A set of engineering constraints we hold ourselves to, because they are what make software still feel good ten years after it shipped.

00 / 07 — seven, held to

  1. 01

    Local First

    Your data lives on your machine. The network is an enhancement, never a requirement.

  2. 02

    Privacy First

    No tracking, no profiling, no quiet collection. What happens on your device stays there.

  3. 03

    Fast by Design

    Performance is a design decision made early, not an optimisation pass added late.

  4. 04

    Minimal Dependencies

    Every dependency is a liability we inherit. We take on very few, and we choose them slowly.

  5. 05

    Long-Term Maintainability

    Written to be understood years from now, by whoever opens the file next.

  6. 06

    User Ownership

    Open formats, easy export, no lock-in. You should be able to leave at any moment.

  7. 07

    Simplicity over Complexity

    The hard work is deciding what not to build. We do that work before writing code.

03 — About
